# Hi, I'm Elliot Little 👋

I lead AI product and deployment: evals, safeguarding and agents in production.

Most of my work is hands-on: product calls, code, evals, and the safeguards around a model that talks to real people. At Zero Gravity I led six product engineers and a designer on an A-Level AI STEM tutor built on Claude, with each engineer owning a problem from prototype to production. I wrote 28% of the commits. It went from a 30-school beta into 850+ UK schools, and the government picked it for its [AI Tutoring Tools Pioneers Programme](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/edtech-and-ai-companies-invited-to-help-build-safe-ai-tutoring-tools-for-disadvantaged-pupils): eight providers chosen from 53 bids.

Everything I build and write lives on [ElliotOS](https://www.elliotos.com/), a personal site run like a product: live projects, a changelog kept by agents, and the evals that check them.

**What I work on**

- AI in production: the [Zero Gravity AI STEM tutor](https://www.zerogravity.co.uk/tutor). Four subjects, eight exam boards, Socratic by design. Every release passed a golden set of real past papers and official mark schemes: bare model 67%, retrieval 79%, our covered content 99%+. I open-coded production traces into failure modes (sycophancy on "I don't know", inconsistency across modes, hallucinated marks) and turned them into a pass/fail LLM judge on every session
- Safety layers for AI that reaches people who can't afford a wrong answer: [ward](https://github.com/ElliotJLT/ward), a UK safeguarding layer for LLM apps serving under-18s, eval set included
- Prototypes for people who can't afford a wrong answer: [lease-citations](https://github.com/ElliotJLT/lease-citations), where every quote is located in the lease before it renders as evidence, and [persona-interviews](https://github.com/ElliotJLT/persona-interviews), interviews with synthetic survey respondents where every claim shows whether it came from the data or the model
- Human judgement inside agent workflows: [crux](https://github.com/ElliotJLT/crux), a field study of what you still decide when the agent writes the code, and [dog-years](https://github.com/ElliotJLT/dog-years), experiments on why agents estimate in human weeks
- How I split my own work with machines: an open-loop system built on Claude Code that holds capture, reminders and follow-ups so I keep the calls. It publishes a weekly ledger that can show it failing, on [/loops](https://www.elliotos.com/loops/)
- Claude Code tooling I use myself: [Skill Potions](https://github.com/ElliotJLT/Claude-Skill-Potions) (skills and hooks) and an [MCP server](https://github.com/ElliotJLT/homebuyer-mcp) for the UK home-buying journey, built on live SRA, FCA and Companies House data
- Career ops as software: [boulot](https://github.com/ElliotJLT/boulot-os), the local system that ran my own search. Three adversarial agents argue over every application before it goes out
- Current experiment: [duty-desk](https://github.com/ElliotJLT/duty-desk), a simulated AI duty desk for small-group tour operators, from the crisis ops I ran at Flash Pack. Write-up coming on ElliotOS
